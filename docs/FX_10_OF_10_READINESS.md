# FX 10/10 Readiness Gates

Ten out of ten means independently verified production readiness. It does not
mean guaranteed profit or forced model accuracy.

| Area | Evidence required for 10/10 |
|---|---|
| Architecture | Versioned contracts and migrations, idempotent workflows, reproducible deployment, no duplicate authorities, 30-day reliability run |
| Data | Verified corporate actions, point-in-time universes, revision-aware macro data, cross-source checks, licensing and quarantine workflows |
| History | Ten years of quality daily core-market data plus strategy-appropriate intraday/quote depth |
| Bias protection | Purged validation, embargo, locked holdout, complete trial ledger, Deflated Sharpe, PBO, Monte Carlo and independent replay |
| Bots | Positive net expectancy with adequate trades/regimes, calibrated confidence, stable OOS and paper behavior |
| Replay | Deterministic event replay through the same signal, risk, order and portfolio contracts used by paper execution |
| Microstructure | Verified spread, slippage, latency, liquidity, partial-fill, session, roll and funding behavior where required |
| Risk | Enforced exits, portfolio/currency/correlation/concentration limits, daily/weekly loss gates, drawdown controls and reconciliation |
| Operations | 60–90 protected paper days, monitored freshness, recovery tests, security review and zero safety bypasses |

Raw profit cannot satisfy these gates. A strategy that fails robustness remains
research evidence or enters the strategy graveyard.
