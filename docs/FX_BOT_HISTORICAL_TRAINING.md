# FX Bot Historical Training

FX distinguishes model fitting from trading qualification. Historical exams
currently measure development, validation, and locked-test behavior under
5/10/20 bps cost assumptions. Recorded metrics include total return, CAGR,
Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, maximum drawdown, profit factor, expectancy, payoff
ratio, volatility, 5% tail loss, turnover, exposure, trades, and sample size.

Every current exam is `RESEARCH_ONLY` and `NOT_ELIGIBLE`. A bot cannot promote
itself. Required next gates are parameter stability, Monte Carlo,
multiple-testing adjustment, independent replay, protected paper examination,
and portfolio risk review. Failures stay in the experiment ledger as useful
negative evidence.
