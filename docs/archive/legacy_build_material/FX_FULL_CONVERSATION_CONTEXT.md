# 1. PROJECT GOAL

FX is a real, personal, local-first AI quantitative trading application.

The intended experience is a ChatGPT-like trading operating system where the user can type questions such as:

- “What is the best stock in the market right now?”
- “Why do you prefer Apple over Nvidia?”
- “Show me the Fibonacci levels.”
- “What method is the model using?”
- “Show me where the strategy would enter.”
- “Compare this setup with historical analogues.”
- “Paper trade this if price reaches X.”
- “How is the forecast proceeding compared with reality?”

FX should respond conversationally, but its answers must be backed by real market data, deterministic calculations, quantitative models, validation evidence, and reproducible research.

The app is intended to support:

- US equities
- ETFs
- crypto
- FX
- gold / metals
- futures
- options research later
- macro research
- portfolio construction
- quantitative strategy research
- backtesting
- paper trading
- shadow live
- controlled live trading later

The app is not a public SaaS product at the beginning. It is a hardened personal quantitative operating system.

# 2. FUNDAMENTAL SAFETY PRINCIPLES

Automated AI trading exposes capital to severe risk.

Machine-learning models can fail under regime shifts.
Backtests can overfit.
Execution can differ from simulation because of:

- slippage
- latency
- partial fills
- rejected orders
- disconnected APIs
- broker outages
- exchange outages
- data errors
- bugs
- unexpected spread changes
- market-impact differences
- margin changes
- structural market regime changes

Therefore:

> Backtested performance never guarantees future live results.

FX must explicitly reward:

> **NO TRADE**

as a valid and desirable result.

A model is not trusted because it is:

- sophisticated
- popular
- recently released
- generated by an LLM
- published in a paper
- profitable in one backtest
- recommended by another AI

Trust is earned only through reproducible evidence under FX’s validation protocol.

# 3. CAPITAL / BROKER ARCHITECTURE

FX should never custody user funds.

Money remains at the broker or exchange.

Example:

```text
Your money
   ↓

   ↓
Broker API
   ↓
FX Execution Service
   ↑
Deterministic Risk Engine
   ↑
Human Approval
   ↑
FX AI / Quant Models
```

FX reads:

- cash
- equity
- buying power
- open positions
- pending orders
- fills
- PnL
- margin state

from the broker API.

FX itself should not hold the funds.

# 4. HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP LIVE TRADING

Human permission is required before any live entry.

AI is allowed to:

- research
- analyze
- generate hypotheses
- compare models
- propose setups
- explain evidence
- generate paper trades
- create live trade proposals

AI is not allowed to:

- silently place live trades
- change live risk limits
- access broker secrets
- disable kill switches
- move money
- withdraw funds
- fabricate human approval

Once a live entry is manually approved, pre-approved protective exits may execute automatically where supported:

- stop-loss
- take-profit
- bracket orders
- OCO
- OTO
- reduce-only exits

Prefer broker-native protective orders where possible.

> **Every generated strategy, failed strategy, model, prediction, approval, order, fill, and reconciliation event is versioned and attributable.**
